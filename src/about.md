## How it started

The Adult Open Court Basketball program began as a small, friends-only group in Modesto and has grown over more than a decade into an open, welcoming run for any adult who wants to play competitive basketball. For the last several years, we've played out of the Riverbank Recreation Department gym, and the program has kept growing the whole time — almost entirely by word of mouth.

## Why we do it this way

Most recreational basketball programs run into the same problems: limited availability, no real coordination, and games that turn into arguments because there's no structure. We built CVSA to fix that. Every session has attendance managed, game flow coordinated, and clear rules enforced — so instead of street-ball chaos, you get a consistent, well-run game every time.

## Who it's for

CVSA is open to adults 15 and up, at every level from weekend regulars to former high school and college players. Most of our regulars already have gym memberships elsewhere, but come back to CVSA specifically for the competitive, full-court cardio workout — a 30-minute full-court run can burn as much as 700 calories, more than most machines in a typical gym.

## Structure

Games run twice a week — Thursday evenings and Sunday mornings through afternoon — split across full-court and half-court groups so players can pick the pace that fits them. Sessions run in four-month blocks, with sign-ups at the start of each block to keep game days manageable for everyone.
