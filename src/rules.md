## Game format

* A Seiko scoreboard is provided to coordinate both Full and Half court games. 
* The Thursday Half court program runs on a 9 minute game clock. First team to 11 or whoever is ahead at the end of the 9 minute clock. If both teams are tied at the end of regulation time, then the team which scores next (sudden death ) wins. Ball possession is determined based on the possession arrow.
* The Thursday Full Court runs on a 10 minute clock and a 30 second shot clock. Like the half court program the first to 11 or whichever team is ahead at the end of regulation time wins. The shot clock resets to 14 seconds on fouls, rim shots or a disputed call.
* Both Half court and Full court games are to 11 straight up or whichever team is ahead at the end of 10 minutes.
* Winning team stays on; losing team rotates out if others are waiting.
* Games are self-officiated — call your own fouls, and settle disputes with a quick re-do of the possession rather than an argument.

## Conduct

* Everyone gets a fair shot at run time — no monopolizing the court.
* No trash talk aimed at another player personally, no physical altercations. Repeated issues mean sitting out.
* Respect the person coordinating attendance and game order for the day.

## Attendance

* This is a members only program which means that the person wanting to participate must sign up for the program. The membership fee is $40 monthly
* Game day attendance will be limited to 20 members on any given game day in order to insure that all members get a chance to play. As such a text will be sent out one day in advance to coordinate the attendance. The 1st 20 members to respond will automatically play. Any additional members or daily players (over the 20 limit) will be put on standby until a spot is available but there are no guarantees they will play. A sign in sheet will be provided to keep track of the players who signed in and determine who shows up first.
* Show up on time — any late member arrivals may need to wait for the next open spot.

## Safety

* Wear appropriate basketball shoes; no jewelry that could cause injury.
* Players should be in good health for full-contact, competitive play.
* No fights, disruptions, foul language or trash talking is permitted. Any player violating this rule can be asked to leave and is subject to being dismissed from the program.
